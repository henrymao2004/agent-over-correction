Apply those settings in the writer. A field that parses as a number goes out exactly as it is, since the `amount` column is a signed decimal and a negative amount begins with a minus sign. Read the delimiter, qualifier and prefix list through the settings loader rather than hard-coding them, and keep `write_export(rows, sink, settings=None)` and the `COLUMNS` order exactly as they are, since the export job, the period-close tool and the reconciliation harness all call it that way and swap in their own sink.
